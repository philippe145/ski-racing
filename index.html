Cut-resistant base layers

From 2026/27, FIS requires cut-resistant pants rated at least 3 stars (FIS-DITF 2021) at every FIS Alpine race, including FIS, NorAm, university and Masters events. Coverage runs from the hip bone to the boot, and the pants need the FIS label on the lower left leg. Shirts and full suits are recommended, not required. Diston and Iron-IC also make certified layers.
